War Game Session Lifecycle
Seven stages under human Orchestrator control — Guardian Engine state machine
1
Init
Session loaded; personas and baseline Why weights seeded.
▶
2
Opening Round
Each persona introduces its role, priorities, and read of the scenario.
▶
3
Status Rounds
Concise, Why-weighted position statements; rounds repeat.
▶
4
Injection Event
Orchestrator fires a White or Red event to all personas.
▼
5
Why Shift
Personas re-weight priorities; dashboard bars reorder within 500 ms.
▶
6
COA Deliberation
Personas reason from updated priorities toward courses of action.
▶
7
Session Conclusion
Each persona ties its final Why ranking to a recommended COA.
Stages 3–5 repeat each round; the Orchestrator injects events and ends the session on command.
An Injection Event and the resulting Why Shift are the dynamic core of the demonstration — every stage advances under human Orchestrator control.
