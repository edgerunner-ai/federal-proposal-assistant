# Distribution Statements — Quick Reference

## Statement A — Public Release
> Distribution Statement A: Approved for public release; distribution is unlimited.

**Use when:** General white papers, marketing materials, publicly shared technical overviews.

## Statement B — U.S. Government Only
> Distribution Statement B: Distribution authorized to U.S. Government agencies only; [reason]; [date]. Other requests shall be referred to [originating office].

**Reasons:** Foreign Government Information, Proprietary Information, Critical Technology, Test and Evaluation, Contractor Performance Evaluation, Administrative/Operational Use, Software Documentation, Specific Authority.

## Statement C — Government & Contractors
> Distribution Statement C: Distribution authorized to U.S. Government agencies and their contractors; [reason]; [date]. Other requests shall be referred to [originating office].

**Use when:** Proposals requiring contractor team review.

## Statement D — DoD & DoD Contractors
> Distribution Statement D: Distribution authorized to the Department of Defense and U.S. DoD contractors only; [reason]; [date]. Other requests for this document shall be referred to [originating office].

**Use when:** White papers to specific DoD programs. **Recommended default for SOCPAC-type submissions.**

## Statement F — Directed Only
> Distribution Statement F: Further dissemination only as directed by [controlling office] or higher DoD authority; [date].

**Use when:** Highly restricted, single-office distribution.

## CUI Marking (optional, add to header)
> Controlled Unclassified Information (CUI) // FEDCON

**Use when:** Document contains proprietary capabilities, pricing, or sensitive but unclassified technical details.
