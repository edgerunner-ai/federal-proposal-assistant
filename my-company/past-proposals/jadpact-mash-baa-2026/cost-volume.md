## 1. Cost Narrative

EdgeRunner proposes a total cost of $98,450 to execute the work described in the Statement of Work. This estimate covers engineering preparation, demonstration support during the MASH event, and preparation of the required Scientific and Technical Report Summary in accordance with the CDRL.

The effort is structured around three primary work activities:

**Pre-event preparation:** Configure and validate the EdgeRunner containerized AI micro-service and prepare integration interfaces for demonstration scenarios aligned with the TM-BM framework. Includes model configuration, integration testing, and validation of runtime performance on the demonstration platform.

**Event support:** Deploy and operate the system in the MASH environment. Provide direct technical support to government personnel during the experiment — configure demonstration scenarios, support user interaction, and collect performance observations relevant to TM-BM decision workflows.

**Post-event reporting:** Prepare the required Scientific and Technical Report Summary (CDRL DI-MISC-80048), documenting technical approach, demonstration activities, observed results, and recommendations for follow-on development.

Labor represents the majority of program cost due to the engineering expertise required to configure mission-trained models and support the experimental environment. No government-furnished equipment is required — the EdgeRunner platform and trained models are provided as part of the demonstration effort.
