# Ways to Put EdgeRunner AI on Contract

EdgeRunner AI | CAGE 9Z176 | UEI SLZTJUA8DBD3 | NAICS 541511
Partnerships POC: Dave Anderson, Head of Partnerships, dave@edgerunnerai.com

We hold contract vehicles today that let most DoD and federal organizations put us on contract
without building a new instrument. This sheet is meant for your contracting office. If the path
you need is not here, tell us the constraint and we will work it with you.

---

## Vehicles we hold now

**CDAO Tradewinds Solutions Marketplace — Awardable status.** Available to any DoD component. The
Tradewinds Commercial Solutions Opening satisfies competition requirements, so a component can
make a direct award without running a new competition. This is normally our fastest path for AI
and machine learning capability, services, and prototypes. Typical timeline is 30 to 90 days.

**NASA SEWP.** Our products are available through Carahsoft as distributor and Government
Acquisitions Inc. as value-added reseller. Any federal agency can issue a delivery order. SEWP
covers software licenses, hardware, and directly associated services such as installation,
configuration, training, and maintenance. It does not cover research or standalone development.
Typical timeline is 2 to 6 weeks once you have a quote.

**ITES-SW2.** Available through our resellers for Army software requirements.

## Paths your office may already have open

**An existing contract of yours.** If work fits within a statement of work and ceiling you already
hold, a task or delivery order is the fastest route available, often days to weeks. Tell us what
you have and we will tell you honestly whether our scope fits inside it.

**Your component's Commercial Solutions Opening.** Many components run their own CSO under
10 U.S.C. 3458 with published areas of interest, including areas covering artificial intelligence.
If your organization has one, it is usually faster than a laboratory Broad Agency Announcement.

**Simplified acquisition.** For commercial products and services below the simplified acquisition
threshold, your contracting officer has procedures that move in weeks rather than months. This is
often the right answer for a scoped pilot, and it is the practical path for end-of-fiscal-year
funds that must obligate before 30 September.

**Prototype Other Transaction.** Under 10 U.S.C. 4022, directly or through a consortium. A
successfully completed prototype supports a follow-on production award to the same performer
without further competition under 10 U.S.C. 4022(f), which makes this a clean path from pilot to
program.

**Subcontract or teaming.** If another company already holds the contract for work we can support,
we are glad to perform as a subcontractor. Introduce us and we will take it from there.

**Broad Agency Announcement.** We respond to BAAs and welcome them for applied research. Please
plan for the full processing time your contracting office needs, which for laboratory BAAs is
commonly four months or more from white paper to award, separate from the funding cycle.

## Research collaboration without a contract

We hold Cooperative Research and Development Agreements with AFRL and the Army Research Laboratory,
including a Joint Work Statement with ARL, and research partnerships with AI2C and NIWC Atlantic.
A CRADA is a good instrument for joint work, data sharing, and intellectual property protection.
Note that a CRADA cannot transfer funds to a company under 15 U.S.C. 3710a, so it complements a
contract rather than replacing one.

## Four things that help us help you

1. **Which organization holds the funds** for the capability you want.
2. **The appropriation and fiscal year.** Research, operations and maintenance, and procurement
   funds buy different things on different clocks, and it changes which path fits.
3. **Your contracting office and a point of contact there.** We are happy to answer their questions
   directly so nothing waits on a translation through the program office.
4. **Any vehicle you already hold** that we might ride.

## Security and accreditation posture

Stated plainly so your security staff can plan against it:

- Active **IL-6 Interim Authority to Test**.
- **IL-5 Authority to Operate** with the U.S. Space Force.
- We deploy on premises and air-gapped inside your accredited boundary rather than as a cloud
  service. We do **not** hold FedRAMP, SOC 2, or ISO 27001 authorization, and we do not claim them.
  Programs requiring authorization in their own boundary should plan for their own process, and we
  will support it.
