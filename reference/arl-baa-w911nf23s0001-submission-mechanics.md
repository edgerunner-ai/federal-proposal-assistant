## Why there is no FY27 white paper window

There are no submission windows, cutoff dates, or annual due dates under this BAA — for
FY27 or any other year. The parent announcement states it plainly:

> "This BAA is a continuously open announcement valid throughout the period from the date
> of issuance through 20 November 2027, unless announced otherwise."
> — W911NF-23-S-0001 Amendment 4, Section A.6 (Response Dates)

and, on proposals:

> "Proposals may be submitted at any time while this BAA is open."

Section D.4.a ("Submission Dates and Times") sets exactly one date: "Proposals will be
considered until and including the closing date of this announcement." That closing date is
20 November 2027 (SAM.gov "Date Offers Due"); the topic itself closes 19 November 2027.

The practical consequence: **timing is driven by the TPOC's funding cycle, not by a posted
deadline.** Whitepapers are reviewed on receipt and returned with "encouraged to submit a
proposal" or "not encouraged," and that recommendation is explicitly based in part on
"availability of funding." To be in the FY27 money, submit early enough that the TPOC can
line the effort up against FY27 funds — which is a conversation with Dr. Cassenti, not a
date on a page. (Precedent inside the BAA: ARO's targeted AEOP opportunity, also rolling,
advises submitting by 31 August of the prior year to make the next year's funding.)

## The prescribed path

1. Preliminary inquiry with the ARL TPOC to discuss the concept and funding availability.
2. Whitepaper to the TPOC — single PDF by email, **5 pages max**, no prescribed format.
   Must carry: title, submitting individual and organization, research topic name, topic ID
   number, and TPOC name. Content: objective and approach, why it is innovative, how it
   advances the state of the art, a rough-order-of-magnitude cost estimate, and
   biographical qualifications. Mark proprietary data and any restricted technical
   data / computer software.
3. Full proposal. A whitepaper is not a prerequisite — all proposals are reviewed regardless —
   but ARL strongly encourages the whitepaper first, and proposal instructions differ by
   instrument (FAR contract, assistance instrument, or other transaction under 10 U.S.C. 4022).

If no TPOC is listed for a topic, use the ARL Contact Us form with "BAA Inquiry" selected and
the subject line "BAA Whitepaper Submission" plus the topic name and ID.

## Notes and cautions

- **SAM.gov is stale.** The notice (Notice ID W911NF-23-S-0001) shows Updated Published Date
  16 Jul 2024 = Amendment 03. ARL's own site posted **Amendment 4 on 8 Jan 2025**. Work from
  the ARL-hosted PDF, not the SAM.gov attachment set.
- The portal (`cftste.experience.crmforce.mil`) is a JavaScript-rendered Salesforce site —
  it returns nothing to plain HTTP fetches or to search-engine crawlers, which is likely part
  of why the topic and its dates were hard to find from SAM.gov or search.
- Scope is 6.1/6.2 foundational research, with 6.3/6.4 possible "when opportunities arise."
  Instrument type is decided by the Government after proposal review.
- The BAA also carries a catch-all topic, "Support to ARL Foundational Research Competencies,"
  for work that does not map to a published topic but aligns to ARL's competencies.
