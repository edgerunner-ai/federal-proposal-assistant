# CSO Full Proposal (Phase 2)

## Evaluator mindset
Team is already interested (you passed Phase 1). Now they need to justify award and structure the deal. Give them the language to do both.

## Required artifacts
- Expanded technical approach
- Execution plan with milestones and acceptance criteria
- Commercial-item pricing with market rate references
- Data rights assertions (if IP is in scope)
- Relevant experience (not full PPQ)
- Proposed terms: IP, warranty, delivery, payment


## Acquisition guidance

Apply `reference/doctrine/acquisition-guidance-catalog.md` (Applicability Matrix row for this type):

**Tier 2:** **CSO § 3458** commercial pricing — see `pricing-artifacts/cso-commercial.md`.
**Tier 1:** GAO-20-195G four characteristics.
**Tier 3:** cATO/RAISE for fielding/ATO claims (evidence-cited).
**Tier 4:** OMB M-25-21/M-25-22 + DoD AI principles.
**Tier 5:** two-bucket data rights if a deliverable is produced.
