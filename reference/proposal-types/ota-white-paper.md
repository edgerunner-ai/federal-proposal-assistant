# OTA White Paper

## Evaluator mindset
"Is this a prototype worth funding under 10 USC 4022? Does the team include non-traditional contributors? Can they execute fast?"

## Required artifacts
- Prototype concept + technology maturity claim
- Expected outcomes and transition path (follow-on production via 10 USC 4022(f))
- Team composition with non-traditional designation
- ROM range with key cost drivers
- Schedule in months with clear milestones
- Consortium affiliation (if applicable)


## Acquisition guidance

Apply `reference/doctrine/acquisition-guidance-catalog.md` (Applicability Matrix row for this type):

**Tier 2:** OTA authority **10 U.S.C. § 4022** (prototype); transition via § 4022(f). Don't import FAR/DCAA cost expectations.
**Tier 1:** ROM graded against **GAO-20-195G** four characteristics; state method + basis.
**Tier 3/4:** RAISE 2.0 / cATO + DoD AI principles where the prototype touches them.
